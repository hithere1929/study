Virus Reproduction¨Technically intracellular parasites –Do not contain their own machinery for reproduction¨Gain entry into specific host cell ¤Capsid (or spikes of the envelope) adhere to specific receptor sites on the host cell surface. ¨Viral nucleic acid then enters a cell ¤Viral genome codes for production of protein units in the capsid. Host cell makes the subunits to create new virus particles¨Relies on host cell enzymes, ribosomes, transfer RNA (tRNA), and ATP for its own replication¨Mistakes made in reproduction allow for mutations, and possible viral evolution as a result.
