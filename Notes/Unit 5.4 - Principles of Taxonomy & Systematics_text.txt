Systemics Biology¨The higher the category, the more inclusive ¨Organisms in the same domain have general characteristics in common ¨Members of a species share very specific characteristics. ¨The task of creating standardized rules of nomenclature is difficult and has, most recently, been aided by the process of DNA barcoding ¤Compares short fragments of DNA sequences from an unknown organism to a large database of sequences from known organisms.¨Traditionally, taxonomists relied on anatomical data ¤Consortium for the Barcode of Life (CBOL), proposes that all scientists will be able to identify a species with the flick of a handheld scanner.nLike the 11-digit Universal Product Code (UPC) used in a supermarket, DNA is the UPC of organisms on Earth nA DNA–bar-coding device would provide a fast and inexpensive way to catalog organisms.

Phylogeny¨Systematicsis the study of diversity of organisms using information from cellular to population levels ¨One goal of systematics is to determine phylogeny(evolutionary history) of a group ¨Phylogeny is often represented as a phylogenetic tree ¤A diagram indicating lines of descent ¤Each branching point: nIs a divergence from a common ancestor nRepresents an organism that gives rise to two or more new groups¨Classification lists the unique characters of each taxon and is intended to reflect phylogeny ¤Ancestral traits: nPresent in all members of a group, and nPresent in the common ancestor ¤Derived traits: nPresent in some members of a group, but absent in the common ancestor

Phylogeny¨Cladisticsis a way to analyze primitive and derived characters and by the construction of phylogenetic trees called a cladogramon the basis of shared derived characters. ¤Arrange taxa into a cladogram ¨A cladogram is a special type of phylogenetic tree ¤Aclade is an evolutionary branch that includes: nA common ancestor, together with all its descendent species ¤It traces the evolutionary history of the group being studied.¨Cladistsare guided by the principle of parsimony—the minimum number of assumptions is most logical.¤The best cladogram is one in which the fewest number of shared derived characters are left unexplained or that minimizes the number of assumed evolutionary changes. ¨Reliability of cladograms is dependent on the knowledge and skill of an investigator
